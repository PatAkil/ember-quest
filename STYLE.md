# Ember Quest — style card

The visual contract for this game. Read it before any change to the look, sprites,
palette or juice, and edit it in the same milestone when a decision below changes.
It used to be the header comment of `game/main.ts`; the code carries no comments now,
so the art decisions live here. The general rules behind it (palette discipline,
contrast floors, sprite craft, CRT) are in the **ensuring-arcade-visuals** skill.

## The game in one paragraph

A retro roguelike JRPG. No overworld walking, no real-time action: every screen is a
menu or a battle tableau. The hero descends through **four acts** — Crypt → Tundra →
Desert → Holy Temple — each a branching node map (FIGHT / ELITE / REST / LOOT / BOSS)
leading to that act's single boss. Combat is turn-based, one enemy at a time: ATTACK
(free) or a known spell (costs MP); enemies hit back PHYSICAL or MAGIC and carry their
own resists. Every clear pays **skill points**, spent on the LEVEL UP screen (HP / MP /
ATK / MAG / DEF / MDEF / CRIT), and loot is a table of **named items** in six slots —
one item per slot, a new find replaces the old — where every RARE+ item carries a
unique effect (hex dagger, ramping axe, twin bolts, reflect mail, phoenix pendant…).
Permadeath; felling the Seraph wins the run, and the THRESHOLD then offers ASCEND (end in glory) or DESCEND into ever-darker depths. Rules live in `game/sim/`, content in
`game/data.ts` (see `DESIGN.md`); the presentation code only draws them.

## Decisions

| Axis | Decision |
|---|---|
| Palette | **PICO8**. The background is a flat **black void** (`PICO8[0]`); the depth planes are the only thing separating "menu" screens from "battle" screens. |
| Hero | A **crimson knight**, authored 22×27 at px 1 and shown **44×54** in the battle tableau (px 2) and 66×81 as the title prop (px 3): body 8, shade 2, highlight 9, white (7) plume, yellow (10) visor, grey (6/5) blade angled off his right side as its own silhouette island. Legs are 3 cells wide each (2 body + 1 shade) so they hold up at 2×. |
| Sprite craft | Every actor authored at px 1 (normals 14–34 px, elites 16–30, bosses 32–39 wide, at most 34 rows), keyline in the rows, 2–3 body tones plus flat accents, 2-frame breathe / float idles (`game/sprites/`). The tableau draws them at **`ACTOR_PX` = 2** (`game/theme.ts`) — up to 78×68 on screen. Whole-cell scaling is allowed here because combat is turn-based (no hitboxes, nothing moves against the pixel grid), the black keyline stays a crisp 2-px outline on the black void, and the px-1 sprites were a quarter of the stage. Every pop, burst and flash origin is derived from the scaled sprite box (`heroPos`, `enemyPos`, `heroAnchor`, `enemyAnchor`), never a hard-coded offset. The fallen knight is his own authored sprite (`heroFallen`, 54×10): lying on his back in profile, head to the left with the plume trailing on the floor, visor up, boots toes-up, and the blade lying flat on the floor ahead of his feet; drawn centred on x 70, sunk 2 cells into the floor plane. Bosses read clearly larger than their act's normals: the Dark Lord is 39×34 (78×68 on screen), a flared cape in purple 2 with navy (1) fold lines fanning out to a scalloped hem, a red (8) lining edge at the opening, domed grey pauldrons. The Ice Bear is 7/6/13/5 (white back, grey 6 flank, lilac 13 underside band, dark-grey 5 belly line and far-leg shade) with lilac contour lines for the shoulder and the haunch, a snouted head and ear, and four 4-cell legs (near legs grey, far legs lilac). The Ice Wolf is 34×20, 12/13/1 with white (7) highlights: a big head with two ears, a snout and a yellow eye, a white neck ruff, a raised tail, a dithered lower flank and four 3-cell legs (near 12/13, far 13/1). The Scorpion is **orange 9 / brown 4** (red eyes and stinger) so it never merges with the yellow desert sun: one pincer, a segmented body on four stub legs and a five-segment tail curling over its back. **Elites** get a red (8) 1-px rim outside their black keyline (the sprite re-drawn tinted at ±1 px behind itself) and a dark-purple (2) ground glow with a red underline that pokes out past their black shadow — dropped while the foe flashes or dissolves. Normals and bosses get neither. |
| Battle stage | Floor line y 112. Knight centred at x 70 (x 48–92, y 58–112). The command menu and the battle log share a **full-bleed band** (y 118–160, navy with a yellow top keyline and a black line above) so the floor shows as a 6-px lip between stage and band; while the hero chooses, the band holds the intent hint, the command grid and its preview column (see *Battle verbs*); the log speaks at **scale 2** on up to two balanced lines and drops to scale 1 only when it cannot fit. The reward screens never leave the floor bare below y 118. |
| Battle verbs | **Intent** — plate text and band hint share one colour per move: `STRIKES` white 7, `HEXES` pink 14, `WINDS UP` and `HEAVY BLOW` orange 9 (HEAVY swaps to white 7 at 2 Hz — never off), `WARDS` blue 12, `SCORCHES` orange 9, `MENDS` green 11, `STAGGERED` grey 6. The hint is centred at y 121 (≤ 52 chars, plain words, `IT ...`; HEAVY reads `HEAVY BLOW NEXT (~n): GUARD, OR BREAK IT` with n the expected unguarded blow, WINDUP `WINDING UP: HIT NOW, GUARD NEXT TURN`, and an otherwise quiet turn `ATTACKS GLANCE OFF: TRY MAGIC` in orange when ATTACK is resisted and a spell would out-hit it), above a dotted grey (5) rule at y 128. **Menu grid** — 2 columns × 3 rows of 68 px at x 8 / 76, rows at y 131 / 139 / 147 (8-px pitch), the cursor row on the purple bar with `> ` and accent text; unavailable commands `C_FAINT` (`C_DIM` when highlighted); the highlighted TOP cell carries a `C_DIM` `>` (left column) or `<` (right column) at its far end pointing at the other column, so MAGIC and BURST read as reachable; BURST lit in orange 9 once ready. While a HEAVY blow is coming the break verdict replaces that note on ATTACK, MAGIC (the best affordable striking spell) and a ready BURST: `BREAKS` in accent when it breaks the blow, `NO BRK` in orange 9 when it does not (`NO BREAK` does not fit beside `ATTACK` in a 68-px cell); a cell carrying a verdict drops the column arrow so the note sits 4 px in. The banner above keeps the blow size. MAGIC swaps the grid for the spell grid (name left, `nMP` right-aligned in `C_DIM`), 6 per page, `X BACK  1/2` in the preview's last free line. A refusal replaces the hint for 1.4 s in orange. **Ember bar** — 2 px under the hero plate (y 36–37, black backing, grey 5 trough), labelled by a 3×4 flame glyph at its left end (yellow 10 tip, orange 9 body, red 8 base; white tip / yellow body when full) with the bar in the remaining 83 px; orange 9 while filling, yellow 10 with an 8-px white glint sweeping every 1.2 s when full; it drains on its own lagging meter. **Tag strips** — words on 7-px black backings inside each plate's span, 3 px apart: enemy at y 38 (37–43, clear of the tallest boss tops at y 44), hero at y 40 (39–45, below the ember bar; pops never rise above y 46): enemy `WEAK FIRE` (white word + element colour: FIRE 8, WATER 12, THUNDER 10, EARTH 15; both words `C_DIM` while the hero has no attack or spell of that element), `ENRAGED` red 8 once the soft enrage is on, `WARD n` blue, `BURN p Xt` orange (damage per tick and turns left; `BURN -p` when it does not fit), `RES <element>`, affixes red (two or more on their own row 9 px under the strip, all at once); hero `EMBER n%` orange first while BURST is highlighted and not full, `BURN p Xt`, `RIPOSTE` accent, `GUARD` blue, `RAMP n` orange, `BURST READY` accent. **Preview words** — BURST before it is full: `EMBER n%` orange / `X2.5 IGNORES ARMOR` / `FILLS AS YOU FIGHT`; the last line carries the hero's fate before the foe's blow at the least favourable roll — `BURN n KILLS YOU` / `THORNS WILL KILL YOU` in pink 14 when he falls, `BURN n - YOU RISE` / `THORNS - YOU RISE` in orange 9 when a REVIVE charge catches him (pink is kept for a real death); POTION, GUARD and healing spells otherwise end on `THEN YOU BURN -n` (orange) — the tick comes after the action — and every other command shows the line only when the fate is not safe. Every preview line stays inside the 82-px column (`THORNS WILL KILL YOU` is the widest at 81 px). **Auras** (state-driven, drawn over the tableau): GUARD a blue shield arc with a white glint in front of the braced knight (who leans 2 px back and 2 px down); a shown HEAVY leans the foe 5 px back and rings it with a growing orange/yellow pulse plus rising sparks; WARD a rotating dotted blue/white ellipse; BURN rising orange/red motes on the burning actor; STAGGERED three yellow sparkles orbiting the head while the foe sways; RIPOSTE a twinkle on the blade tip. **Beats** — GUARD shield glint + `jump`; a guarded hit `GUARD -n` in blue, 1-px shake, `blip`; PARRY `PARRY!` at scale 3 in accent, white radial flash, 0.14 s hit-stop; the riposte hit pops at scale 3 with a white radial flash; BREAK `BREAK!` (scale 2), recoil 12, white flash, 0.18 s hit-stop; WEAK `WEAK!` green + an element-coloured bloom, 0.1 s hit-stop; RESIST a small grey `RESIST`; HEAVY a hop-lunge, scale-3 number, red radial flash, 6-px shake; WARD a blue ring; MEND a green rise and `+n`; SCORCH an orange orb or blade and a small `BURN` pop; BURN ticks a scale-1 `BURN -n` at the actor's head, 0.45 s after that actor's own action (the log then reads `YOU BURN FOR n` / `<FOE> BURNS FOR n`); the foe's soft enrage puts `IT GROWS IMPATIENT - ITS BLOWS HIT HARDER` in orange in the log after its action; the boss entry heal pops (`+n HP`, `+n MP`) wait until the entrance banner has left; POTION a green flask raised to the visor, green tint and `+n HP`; **BURST** a charge (ring + cross at the blade) then a full-width beam (orange/yellow/white bands) with a uniform yellow flash, 7-px shake, 0.16 s hit-stop and the game's biggest pop (scale 4); phase 2 the banner strip (`PHASE 2` in red + the boss's line), a white-then-red flash on the boss, a roar pose and a 5-px shake. |

## Controls (as presented)

Up / Down (arrows or WASD) move every cursor; **A** confirms, spends a point or
advances; **B** (X/C) goes back and opens GEAR from the map; Left / Right toggle a choice
(the loot screen's EQUIP / STOW); **PAUSE** pauses. The title's hints come from the declared
actions (`select`, `back / gear`, `pause`), filtered to the ones that act there (`select`).
