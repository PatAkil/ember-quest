# Ember Quest — style card

The visual contract for this game. Read it before any change to the look, sprites,
palette or juice, and edit it in the same milestone when a decision below changes.
It used to be the header comment of `game/main.ts`; the code carries no comments now,
so the art decisions live here. The general rules behind it (palette discipline,
contrast floors, sprite craft, CRT) are in the **ensuring-arcade-visuals** skill.

## The game in one paragraph

A retro roguelike JRPG. No overworld walking, no real-time action: every screen is a
menu or a battle tableau. The hero descends through **four acts** — Crypt → Tundra →
Desert → Holy Temple — each a branching node map (FIGHT / ELITE / REST / LOOT / BOSS)
leading to that act's single boss. Combat is turn-based, one enemy at a time: ATTACK
(free) or a known spell (costs MP); enemies hit back PHYSICAL or MAGIC and carry their
own resists. Every clear pays **skill points**, spent on the LEVEL UP screen (HP / MP /
ATK / MAG / DEF / MDEF / CRIT), and loot is a table of **named items** in six slots —
one item per slot, a new find replaces the old — where every RARE+ item carries a
unique effect (hex dagger, ramping axe, twin bolts, reflect mail, phoenix pendant…).
Permadeath; felling the Seraph wins the run. Rules live in `game/sim/`, content in
`game/data.ts` (see `DESIGN.md`); the presentation code only draws them.

## Decisions

| Axis | Decision |
|---|---|
| Palette | **PICO8**. The background is a flat **black void** (`PICO8[0]`); the depth planes are the only thing separating "menu" screens from "battle" screens. |
| Hero | A **crimson knight**, authored 22×27 at px 1 and shown **44×54** in the battle tableau (px 2) and 66×81 as the title prop (px 3): body 8, shade 2, highlight 9, white (7) plume, yellow (10) visor, grey (6/5) blade angled off his right side as its own silhouette island. Legs are 3 cells wide each (2 body + 1 shade) so they hold up at 2×. |
| Sprite craft | Every actor authored at px 1 (normals 14–34 px, elites 16–30, bosses 32–39 wide, at most 34 rows), keyline in the rows, 2–3 body tones plus flat accents, 2-frame breathe / float idles (`game/sprites/`). The tableau draws them at **`ACTOR_PX` = 2** (`game/theme.ts`) — up to 78×68 on screen. Whole-cell scaling is allowed here because combat is turn-based (no hitboxes, nothing moves against the pixel grid), the black keyline stays a crisp 2-px outline on the black void, and the px-1 sprites were a quarter of the stage. Every pop, burst and flash origin is derived from the scaled sprite box (`heroPos`, `enemyPos`, `heroAnchor`, `enemyAnchor`), never a hard-coded offset. The fallen knight is his own authored sprite (`heroFallen`, 54×10): lying on his back in profile, head to the left with the plume trailing on the floor, visor up, boots toes-up, and the blade lying flat on the floor ahead of his feet; drawn centred on x 70, sunk 2 cells into the floor plane. Bosses read clearly larger than their act's normals: the Dark Lord is 39×34 (78×68 on screen), a flared cape in purple 2 with navy (1) fold lines fanning out to a scalloped hem, a red (8) lining edge at the opening, domed grey pauldrons. The Ice Bear is 7/6/13/5 (white back, grey 6 flank, lilac 13 underside band, dark-grey 5 belly line and far-leg shade) with lilac contour lines for the shoulder and the haunch, a snouted head and ear, and four 4-cell legs (near legs grey, far legs lilac). The Ice Wolf is 34×20, 12/13/1 with white (7) highlights: a big head with two ears, a snout and a yellow eye, a white neck ruff, a raised tail, a dithered lower flank and four 3-cell legs (near 12/13, far 13/1). The Scorpion is **orange 9 / brown 4** (red eyes and stinger) so it never merges with the yellow desert sun: one pincer, a segmented body on four stub legs and a five-segment tail curling over its back. **Elites** get a red (8) 1-px rim outside their black keyline (the sprite re-drawn tinted at ±1 px behind itself) and a dark-purple (2) ground glow with a red underline that pokes out past their black shadow — dropped while the foe flashes or dissolves. Normals and bosses get neither. |
| Battle stage | Floor line y 112. Knight centred at x 70 (x 48–92, y 58–112). The enemy's front edge sits at x 136 (centre at least x 162), so the gap between the two is a constant ~44 px for wide foes and up to ~56 px for small ones, and the widest (Seraph, 78) ends at x 214. HUD: two mirrored **96×27 plates** at y 8–35 (hero x 8–104, enemy x 136–232) — row 1 the stat line (`ATK / MAG / CRIT`, falling back to `A / M / C` unless the long form leaves 18 px of air) or the enemy name with its BOSS/ELITE tag right-aligned; row 2 `HP` bar + numbers on both; row 3 the hero's `MP` bar or the enemy's `STRIKES`/`HEXES` intent and resist tag. `RAMP n` hangs under the hero plate (y 38) on a solid black backing so it never sits on a pillar or capital. SCORE is a two-line label/value in the 32-px gap between the plates. The command menu and the battle log share a **full-bleed band** (y 118–160, navy with a yellow top keyline and a black line above) so the floor shows as a 6-px lip between stage and band; menu rows are centred in it (two columns with a grey divider for 4–6 actions; the info text keeps 9 px from the column edge and the cursor bar is inset 3 px, and when a row would come within 7 px of its label the info drops to `x2.1 -25%`, then to the bare multiplier — still orange when resisted), the log speaks at **scale 2** on up to two balanced lines and drops to scale 1 only when it cannot fit. LEVEL UP keeps the knight where he stands (his `+n STAT` pops rise from him) with the panel beside him at x 104–232, y 8–102: scale-2 heading, one stat per row at 10-px pitch, the cursor row on the purple bar. The same command band closes the screen: `n SP TO SPEND` at scale 2 in accent with `+n EARNED` right-aligned, the cursor stat's gain and a plain-words hint, and the blinking prompt. The reward screens never leave the floor bare below y 118. |
| Background planes | Under the ambient layer, inside the ambient band. **Crypt**: `PICO8[1]` pillars at x 14 / 76 / 154 / 216 (behind the actors, never on a silhouette edge), with torch flames only as wall sconces on the two outer pillars (brown cup, grey bracket, at y 56–66) — clear of the HUD panels, the title lines and the card frame. The map draws the same backdrop through `renderBiomeBackdrop(g, dim)`, whose `dim` multiplies every internal alpha (map: 0.45). **Tundra**: `PICO8[1]` crescent moon + ice peaks. **Desert**: a round sun (r 13, centre y 70) floating in the gap between the fighters, drawn **solid** — orange 9 rim, yellow 10 core, a small white glint at upper left, no bands — above brown dune domes at reduced alpha. The desert sun is skipped on the map (any `dim` < 1) so it never clutters the act-3 graph, and on reward/card screens without an enemy. **Temple**: fluted dark-grey columns (alpha 0.7) that start at y 50, below the HUD plates and clear of `RAMP n`, each crowned by a **full-alpha** gold capital (3 rows of 10 over 1 row of 9). |
| Surfaces | `drawBevel` floors — Crypt 5/6/1, Tundra 1/12/0, Desert 4/9/2, Temple 2/13/1 (fill / light / dark — a dark plum floor with a lilac top light: dark enough for white end-screen text, and plum rather than the Crypt's grey; the old 13/7 and 6 fills read as glaring slabs under the CRT lift) — with one dither lip, and black ground shadows under the actors. Panels are **square** dark-blue (1) plates with a yellow (10) keyline; the selected row gets a dark-purple (2) bar. The floor bevel overscans 1 px each side so no edge highlight shows. |
| Text roles | `theme.ts` names them: `C_TEXT` 7, `C_DIM` 6 (7.83:1 on navy), `C_FAINT` 13 (lilac, 5.04:1 on black, 3.31:1 on navy — only for unavailable or empty things: empty slots, unaffordable actions, cleared/unreachable map nodes, map edges), `C_WARN` 9 (orange, 6.89:1 on navy, 4.61:1 on the purple bar — the resist tag), `C_LOG_HURT` 14 (pink, 5.55:1 on navy — the battle log when the hero is struck or falls; red 8 is only 3.52:1 there, so `C_DMG_TAKEN` red stays for the hero's damage pops, which sit on their own black plate at 5.36:1). `PICO8[5]` is fill-only (bar troughs, dividers), never text. Map and gear text: see the *Map*, *Icons*, *Loot* and *Cards* rows. |

Each also has a flat `faint` (13) and `spent` (5) silhouette for visited / passed nodes. **Gear icons 7×7** (9×9 drawn) — sword 7/6, wand (blue orb on a gold shaft), armour 9/10, necklace (pink gem on a gold chain), boots 15, chalice 10, tome 11 (dark-green 3 spine with two gold bands down the left, a white/grey page block on the right and bottom edges, gold emblem on the cover) — the same sprites at px 1 in the status and loot rows and at px 4 on the item cards (black keyline on navy is 1.52:1, so the px-4 band reads as one silhouette). The scroll is the tome in blue (12) with a lilac spine. Empty slot = dashed corners in `C_FAINT`. The hero marker is a 7×7 red helm with white plume and yellow visor. |
| Map | Nodes are **15×15 black medallions** (cut corners) with a 1-px ring and the 9×9 icon inside; no per-node labels — the header's second line reads out the cursor node (type in accent, blurb in `C_DIM`). Ring states: cursor 10 doubled (a second ring 1 px inside) with a 19×19 halo that blinks 10/9 (yellow/orange — never white or grey, so it can never look like a reachable halo), reachable 7 doubled with a 19×19 halo pulsing 6/5 at 1 Hz, walked 10 (icon as a `C_FAINT` silhouette), passed / unreachable 5 (dark-grey silhouette), ahead `C_FAINT` with the full-colour icon. Room hues on the black plate: 7 19.0:1, 8 5.36:1, 9 10.49:1, 12 8.52:1, 14 8.46:1. Stages at x 31 + 32·s, slots 24 px apart around y 63 (3-node stages y 32–94). | Terminal screens | The live tableau (actors and backdrop only — no HUDs, no battle log, no pops) + `dimScene`, with two hollow `drawFrame` boxes that never overlap the actors: the headline in the sky (y 6–35) and the summary + prompt on the floor band (y 116–154). The summary is one line `n CLEARED  SCORE n  BEST n`, then the gear names joined with ` / ` and packed onto at most two lines inside the frame, then the prompt. Dim, never a plate. **PAUSED** is the exception: `dimScene` plus a small navy panel with a yellow keyline (100×32, centred at y 62) holding PAUSED at scale 2 and the resume hint, so the word never lands on backdrop art. |
| Shell and scale | `index.html` is a slim arcade cabinet: "Ember Quest" marquee, one bezel, a light-grey (`#a39c94`) hint — under 80 CSS px of vertical chrome. The 240×160 frame is shown at the **largest whole number** of CSS px per logical px that fits the window or host iframe — down to 1 — centred and re-fit on resize (`autoFit` in `game/app.ts`, `fitDisplay` in `engine/canvas.ts`), so every logical pixel lands on whole CSS pixels. Only below 240×160 does it CSS-shrink (fractionally) so nothing is ever clipped; there (under 300 px wide or 260 px tall) the shell drops the marquee, hint and bezel padding. The **backing store is capped at 6×** (1440×960) because the CRT pass covers all of it every frame; past the cap the displayed scale is the backing × a whole-number CSS enlargement under `image-rendering: pixelated` (backing kept in 3–6×, largest product first, then largest backing: 13 → 6×2 = 12, 8 → 4×2, 7 → 6×1). Hold mode (`?hold=1`, the screenshot tooling) pins it at 3× (720×480) with no CSS sizing. |

## Controls (as presented)

Up / Down (arrows or WASD) move every cursor; **A** confirms, spends a point or
advances; **B** (X/C) goes back and opens GEAR from the map; Left / Right toggle a choice
(the loot screen's EQUIP / STOW); **PAUSE** pauses. The title's hints come from the declared
actions (`select`, `back / gear`, `pause`).
