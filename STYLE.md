# Ember Quest — style card

The visual contract for this game. Read it before any change to the look, sprites,
palette or juice, and edit it in the same milestone when a decision below changes.
It used to be the header comment of `game/main.ts`; the code carries no comments now,
so the art decisions live here. The general rules behind it (palette discipline,
contrast floors, sprite craft, CRT) are in the **ensuring-arcade-visuals** skill.

## The game in one paragraph

A retro roguelike JRPG. No overworld walking, no real-time action: every screen is a
menu or a battle tableau. The hero descends through **four acts** — Crypt → Tundra →
Desert → Holy Temple — each a branching node map (FIGHT / ELITE / REST / LOOT / BOSS)
leading to that act's single boss. Combat is turn-based, one enemy at a time: ATTACK
(free) or a known spell (costs MP); enemies hit back PHYSICAL or MAGIC and carry their
own resists. Every clear pays **skill points**, spent on the LEVEL UP screen (HP / MP /
ATK / MAG / DEF / MDEF / CRIT), and loot is a table of **named items** in six slots —
one item per slot, a new find replaces the old — where every RARE+ item carries a
unique effect (hex dagger, ramping axe, twin bolts, reflect mail, phoenix pendant…).
Permadeath; felling the Seraph wins the run. Rules live in `game/sim/`, content in
`game/data.ts` (see `DESIGN.md`); the presentation code only draws them.

## Decisions

| Axis | Decision |
|---|---|
| Palette | **PICO8**. The background is a flat **black void** (`PICO8[0]`); the depth planes are the only thing separating "menu" screens from "battle" screens. |
| Hero | A **crimson knight**, authored 22×27 at px 1 and shown **44×54** in the battle tableau (px 2) and 66×81 as the title prop (px 3): body 8, shade 2, highlight 9, white (7) plume, yellow (10) visor, grey (6/5) blade angled off his right side as its own silhouette island. |
| Sprite craft | Every actor authored at px 1 (normals 14–38 px, elites 14–25, bosses 26–39 wide, at most 34 rows), keyline in the rows, 2–3 body tones plus flat accents, 2-frame breathe / float idles (`game/sprites/`). The tableau draws them at **`ACTOR_PX` = 2** (`game/theme.ts`) — up to 78×68 on screen. Whole-cell scaling is allowed here because combat is turn-based (no hitboxes, nothing moves against the pixel grid), the black keyline stays a crisp 2-px outline on the black void, and the px-1 sprites were a quarter of the stage. Every pop, burst and flash origin is derived from the scaled sprite box (`heroPos`, `enemyPos`, `heroAnchor`, `enemyAnchor`), never a hard-coded offset. The fallen knight reuses his own pixels: the blade cells are cut out, the body is laid flat (head to the left) sunk 3 cells into the floor plane, the blade drops on the floor ahead of his feet. |
| Battle stage | Floor line y 112. Knight centred at x 70 (x 48–92, y 58–112). The enemy's front edge sits at x 136 (centre at least x 162), so the gap between the two is a constant ~44 px for wide foes and up to ~56 px for small ones, and the widest (Seraph, 78) ends at x 214. HUD: two mirrored **96×27 plates** at y 8–35 (hero x 8–104, enemy x 136–232) — row 1 the stat line (`ATK / MAG / CRIT`, falling back to `A / M / C` when wide) or the enemy name with its BOSS/ELITE tag right-aligned; row 2 `HP` bar + numbers on both; row 3 the hero's `MP` bar or the enemy's `STRIKES`/`HEXES` intent and resist tag. `RAMP n` hangs under the hero plate (y 38). SCORE is a two-line label/value in the 32-px gap between the plates. The command menu and the battle log share a **full-bleed band** (y 118–160, navy with a yellow top keyline and a black line above) so the floor shows as a 6-px lip between stage and band; menu rows are centred in it (two columns with a grey divider for 4–6 actions, compact info when a row would collide), the log speaks at **scale 2** on up to two balanced lines and drops to scale 1 only when it cannot fit. LEVEL UP keeps the knight where he stands (his `+n STAT` pops rise from him) with the panel beside him at x 104–232, y 8–108: scale-2 heading, one stat per row, the cursor row on the purple bar. |
| Background planes | Under the ambient layer, inside the ambient band. **Crypt**: `PICO8[1]` pillars at x 14 / 76 / 154 / 216 (behind the actors, never on a silhouette edge), with torch flames only as wall sconces on the two outer pillars (brown cup, grey bracket, at y 56–66) — clear of the HUD panels, the title lines and the card frame. The map draws the same backdrop through `renderBiomeBackdrop(g, dim)`, whose `dim` multiplies every internal alpha (map: 0.45). **Tundra**: `PICO8[1]` crescent moon + ice peaks. **Desert**: a low orange sun set in the gap between the fighters, behind brown dune domes at reduced alpha. **Temple**: fluted dark-grey columns with gold capitals. |
| Surfaces | `drawBevel` floors — Crypt 5/6/1, Tundra 1/12/0, Desert 4/9/2, Temple 6/7/5 (fill / light / dark) — with one dither lip, and black ground shadows under the actors. Panels are **square** dark-blue (1) plates with a yellow (10) keyline; the selected row gets a dark-purple (2) bar. The floor bevel overscans 1 px each side so no edge highlight shows. |
| Text roles | `theme.ts` names them: `C_TEXT` 7, `C_DIM` 6 (7.83:1 on navy), `C_FAINT` 13 (lilac, 5.04:1 on black, 3.31:1 on navy — only for unavailable or empty things: empty slots, unaffordable actions, cleared/unreachable map nodes, map edges), `C_WARN` 9 (orange, 6.89:1 on navy, 4.61:1 on the purple bar — the resist tag). `PICO8[5]` is fill-only (bar troughs, dividers), never text. Map and gear text: see the *Map*, *Icons*, *Loot* and *Cards* rows. |
| Icons | Authored at px 1 in `game/sprites/icons.ts`, 2–3 tones, black keyline baked by `makeSprite(..., { outline: PICO8[0] })` (footprint = authored size + 2). **Room icons 9×9** (11×11 drawn), one silhouette and one hue family each: FIGHT crossed swords (white/grey steel 7/6, orange guards), ELITE horned skull (red 8, yellow eyes, bone horns), REST campfire (orange/yellow 9/10 on brown logs), LOOT chest (blue 12 with gold straps), BOSS horned crown (pink 14, gold and blue gems). Each also has a flat `faint` (13) and `spent` (5) silhouette for visited / passed nodes. **Gear icons 7×7** (9×9 drawn) — sword 7/6, wand (blue orb on a gold shaft), armour 9/10, necklace (pink gem on a gold chain), boots 15, chalice 10, tome 11 — the same sprites at px 1 in the status and loot rows and at px 4 on the item cards (black keyline on navy is 1.52:1, so the px-4 band reads as one silhouette). The scroll is the tome in blue (12). Empty slot = dashed corners in `C_FAINT`. The hero marker is a 7×7 red helm with white plume and yellow visor. |
| Map | Nodes are **15×15 black medallions** (cut corners) with a 1-px ring and the 9×9 icon inside; no per-node labels — the header's second line reads out the cursor node (type in accent, blurb in `C_DIM`). Ring states: cursor 10 with a 19×19 halo that blinks 10/7 (never off), reachable 7, walked 10 (icon as a `C_FAINT` silhouette), passed / unreachable 5 (dark-grey silhouette), ahead `C_FAINT` with the full-colour icon. Room hues on the black plate: 7 19.0:1, 8 5.36:1, 9 10.49:1, 12 8.52:1, 14 8.46:1. Stages at x 31 + 32·s, slots 24 px apart around y 63 (3-node stages y 32–94). Edges are straight 1-px lines from port to port, fanned ±3 px at each end; draw order past (5) → ahead (13) → the cursor node's onward preview (6) → the walked path (10) → choices (7) → the cursor's edge (10), every lit edge over a 1-px black casing so crossings stay traceable. The hero helm stands on the last cleared node (or in an accent start medallion left of stage 0). Status panel (y 106–151): gear in two columns × three rows at 9-px pitch (icon + name ≤ 63 px), HP / MP gauges in a right column after a grey divider, and the stat line as label (`C_DIM`) / value (`C_TEXT`) pairs spread across the width. |
| Loot | One panel at the top (x 8–231): stacked rows at 10-px pitch — icon, rarity-coloured name (≤ 71 px, "SCROLL OF TWINBOLT"), the slot / compare hint in `C_DIM`, the rarity / kind tag right-aligned — then the **item card** for the selected row: a 40×40 black medallion ringed in the rarity colour with the icon at px 4, the name at scale 2, the stat lines, the effect blurb in its colour and the compare line in `C_DIM`. KEEP YOUR GEAR shows the campfire at px 3 in a green ring. The panel ends above the floor (≤ y 110 with three offers). |
| Cards | `dimScene(0.6)` over the tableau, then a hollow double frame (yellow outer, purple inner) at x 24–215, y 6–55 — in the sky, above the 2× hero (y 58+). Text cards: title at scale 2 in accent (scale 1 when it would not fit), a short orange rule, the body line, a blinking prompt. Item cards (equip / upgrade / awaken / scroll): the icon at px 4 in a 40×40 rarity-ringed medallion on the left, then the kicker in `C_DIM`, the name at scale 2 in the rarity (scroll: blue) colour, the detail line and the prompt. No opaque plate. |
| Rarity language | Item names tint by rarity: grey / blue / pink / yellow (COMMON / RARE / EPIC / LEGENDARY). |
| Logo | `drawLogo` with color 10, shade 9, shadow 2, at y 10. The title prop is the knight at **px 3** (x 19–85) on the Crypt floor with a slime at px 2 idling opposite under the copy column; the hook lines are centred on that column (x 160, y 38/48) so no text crosses a sprite. |
| Accent | Warm yellow (10) for headings, the selected row and borders. |
| Ambient | Crypt embers · Tundra snow · Desert embers as dust (peach, 15) · Temple bubbles as motes (gold, 10). |
| Juice | Shake + yellow flash on hits; red shake + flash when struck; damage/heal pops spawn above the struck actor's head (from the scaled sprite height) with a solid black backing, never higher than y 49 so their 11-px rise stops below the HUD plates (y 38 is a hard ceiling); when that clamp bites (tall foes) the pop slides toward the actor's front edge, into the gap; simultaneous pops (multi-hit, regen + heal) take slots 16 px apart with an 8-px stagger, and every pop is kept inside `SAFE_MARGIN` — scale 2 with a black shadow for normal hits, scale 3 with a black outline for crits; crits also get a hit-stop that holds the battle queue; bursts in each actor's own hue; on the killing blow a red **radial** flash from the hero plus a freeze-frame — the knight falls where he stood and the killer stays under the dim. |
| Terminal screens | The live tableau (actors and backdrop only — no HUDs, no battle log, no pops) + `dimScene`, with two hollow `drawFrame` boxes that never overlap the actors: the headline in the sky (y 6–35) and the summary + prompt on the floor band (y 116–154). Dim, never a plate. |
| Shell and scale | `index.html` is a slim arcade cabinet: "Ember Quest" marquee, one bezel, a light-grey (`#a39c94`) hint — under 80 CSS px of vertical chrome. The 240×160 frame is scaled by the **largest whole integer** (min 2) that fits the window or host iframe in CSS px (`autoFit` in `game/app.ts`), re-fit on resize, never CSS-stretched; hold mode (`?hold=1`, the screenshot tooling) pins it at 3× (720×480). |

## Controls (as presented)

Up / Down (arrows or WASD) move every cursor; **A** confirms, spends a point or
advances; **PAUSE** pauses. **B is intentionally undeclared** — the title's hints come
from the declared actions (`select`, `pause`).
